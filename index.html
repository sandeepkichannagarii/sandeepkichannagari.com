About Me

I'm a software engineer based in Henrico, Virginia, with a focus on full-stack web development, cloud architecture, and building products that scale.

Over the years I've had the privilege of working across the full product lifecycle — from early-stage ideation and architecture to shipping production systems used by thousands of people. I care deeply about code quality, team culture, and building things that genuinely work.

My technical toolkit spans JavaScript/TypeScript, Python, React, Node.js, and AWS. But beyond the stack, what drives me is the craft — writing code that's readable, systems that are maintainable, and software that people actually enjoy using.

Outside of work, I'm an active member of the Richmond/Henrico tech community, a chess enthusiast, a hiker of Virginia's trails, and an amateur cook specializing in South Asian cuisine.
